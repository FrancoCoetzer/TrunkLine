# Code of conduct

TrunkLine is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it (issues,
pull requests, discussions) is expected to make it a pleasant place to be.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all. Everyone was new once.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Disagree about ideas and code, never about people. Say what you'd change
  and why.
- Keep it on topic, and keep private things private: don't post other
  people's personal details, or anyone's company data.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone, for any reason:
  who they are, where they're from, how experienced they are, or anything
  else.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm
  themselves or others.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or repeatedly pushing a topic after
  being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer, Franco Coetzer, privately: through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/FrancoCoetzer/TrunkLine/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches the maintainer only, and works for conduct reports too), or by
contacting [@FrancoCoetzer](https://github.com/FrancoCoetzer). Reports are
read, kept confidential, and answered.

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask someone to stop, edit or
remove comments, or block someone from the project, temporarily or for good.

This applies in all of the project's spaces on GitHub, and wherever someone
represents the project.
